## Appeal text — use after the prerequisites above are met

Subject: Request for reconsideration — SyndeoCare — Guideline 3.1.1

Dear App Review Board,

We respectfully request reconsideration of the Guideline 3.1.1 rejection of SyndeoCare. We have already replied to the review team's rejection message and are providing additional clarification and supporting evidence.

SyndeoCare connects healthcare professionals with healthcare facilities for real-world, in-person healthcare employment and shifts. Both professional and facility accounts are free. There are no paid digital features, subscriptions, in-app purchases, external purchases, or previously purchased content to access. No payment is required to create a facility account, publish opportunities, review applications, or communicate with candidates, subject to verification and fair-use limits.

During our investigation we found inconsistent legacy wording on our website describing a “30-day trial,” “non-renewable” access and a “free starter plan.” We also found a legacy time-based access restriction. We have removed the time-based restriction and corrected the website and shared legal terms to reflect the current free service. Facility access continues free after 30 days. No payment or purchase is required to continue, renew access, unlock a feature, or increase an allowance. Fair-use limits are not monetized, and verification and privacy protections remain unchanged. We acknowledge that the earlier description could have caused confusion; we do not assume it was the review team's sole reason for rejection.

Salary and hourly compensation shown in listings refer to physical healthcare work agreed between the facility and the professional outside SyndeoCare. These amounts are not charges for digital content or app access. SyndeoCare does not collect or process those payments.

Our primary clarification regarding Guideline 3.1.1 is that there is no digital feature or content unlocked in exchange for payment. Guideline 3.1.3(e) provides additional context for real-world services consumed outside the app. We also correct our earlier mistaken reference to 3.1.5(e); that was not the applicable reference for this service.

The attached evidence shows the corrected public information and the facility journey in the submitted app without payment. Existing review credentials are provided securely in App Review Information.

Please reconsider the current submission. If a specific screen or workflow still raises a concern under Guideline 3.1.1, please identify it so we can address it precisely.

Thank you for your time,
Abdullah Alwahdi
SyndeoCare
